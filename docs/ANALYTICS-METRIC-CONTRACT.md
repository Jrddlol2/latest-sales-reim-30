# Analytics Metric Contract

This document defines the financial and workflow measures that dashboards and
exports must use. It exists to keep role-specific analytics consistent and to
prevent requested, approved, and released money from being mixed together.

## Financial amounts

| Metric | Definition | Reimbursement | Cash advance | Liquidation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claimed amount | Amount submitted or reported by the requestor | Claim total | Advance requested | Total expenses reported |
| Approved amount | Amount accepted by the approver/reviewer | Approved claim total | Approved advance amount | Reviewed/closed expense total |
| Paid amount | Cash actually released by the company | Set when moved to Ready for Claim | Set when moved to Released | Always zero; the related advance or shortfall reimbursement owns the cash movement |
| Outstanding amount | Approved amount minus paid amount | Approved but not released | Approved but not released | Not applicable |

Rules:

- Never label claimed amount as expenditure or paid spend.
- Never add liquidation totals to cash-advance paid amounts as one combined paid
  total; that would count the same funds twice.
- Reimbursement shortfalls generated by liquidation are normal reimbursement
  claims and own their subsequent approved and paid amounts.
- `Claim.total` remains a compatibility alias for claimed amount. New analytics
  must use the explicit amount fields.

## Lifecycle timestamps

Lifecycle timestamps come from the immutable status history unless a dedicated
stored timestamp exists.

| Timestamp | Reimbursement event | Cash advance event | Liquidation event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Submitted | Pending Approval | Submitted | Submitted |
| Approved/reviewed | Processing / `approved_at` | Approved | Reviewed or Closed |
| Paid/released | Ready for Claim / `paid_at` | Released / `releaseDate` | Not applicable |
| Completed | Completed | Liquidated | Closed |

Cycle-time calculations must require both endpoints. Missing timestamps produce
`No data`, not a zero duration and not a `createdAt` substitute.

## Date filters

Every analytics screen must state which date it filters:

- Filed: submitted timestamp
- Expense: line-item expense date
- Approved: approved/reviewed timestamp
- Paid: paid/released timestamp
- Completed: terminal status timestamp

If a record does not have the selected date, it is excluded from that
date-based result.

## Receipt measures

- Supported expense amount: total of filtered line items with a receipt.
- Receipt coverage: filtered line items with a receipt divided by all filtered
  applicable line items.
- Average receipt value: supported expense amount divided by receipt count.
- Official receipt number is a searchable business field and is not a filename.

## Status and scope

- Role visibility is applied before aggregation.
- Drafts are excluded from Finance analytics.
- Team scope uses the current reporting hierarchy and active delegations where
  applicable.
- Status history for reimbursements, cash advances, and liquidations must all be
  loaded before workflow metrics are calculated.
